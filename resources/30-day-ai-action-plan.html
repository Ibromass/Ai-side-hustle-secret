Your 30-Day AI Action Plan

One small step per day to turn learning into practice. Move at a pace that fits your life.

Before you begin: choose one service, use practice or permissioned information, and verify current AI tool terms. This plan is for learning. It does not guarantee income.

Day 1Pick one service from the book and write why it interests you.
Day 2List what you already know and what you need to learn.
Day 3Check current tool access, pricing, terms and privacy practices.
Day 4Study the relevant chapter and note three useful ideas.
Day 5Practise one small task using sample content.
Day 6Review your practice output for accuracy and clarity.
Day 7Reflect on what felt easy and what needs work.
Day 8Choose a customer type and learn about its needs.
Day 9Write five open-ended questions for potential customers.
Day 10Have or plan one respectful customer conversation.
Day 11Summarize needs without guessing or overclaiming.
Day 12Define one small deliverable that may help.
Day 13List scope, exclusions, timeline and client inputs.
Day 14Make a clearly labeled sample deliverable.
Day 15Ask a trusted reviewer for specific feedback.
Day 16Improve the sample based on useful feedback.
Day 17Draft a plain-language service description.
Day 18Estimate your time and costs before setting a price.
Day 19Prepare a short proposal outline.
Day 20Create a portfolio note that labels sample work honestly.
Day 21Make a small, relevant prospect list.
Day 22Personalize one outreach message.
Day 23Send a respectful message and track the next step.
Day 24Review the response and note what you learned.
Day 25Improve your offer or sample based on feedback.
Day 26Create a post explaining one useful lesson.
Day 27Share an accurate post with a practical takeaway.
Day 28Review your work and update your checklist.
Day 29Choose your next skill or customer conversation.
Day 30Write a reflection and a realistic next-month plan.

Day 30 reflection

What did you practise? What feedback did you receive? What is one realistic next action?

Keep your notes private. Do not share sensitive customer data with AI tools without a lawful basis and a clear understanding of their data practices.
