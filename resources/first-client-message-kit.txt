FIRST CLIENT MESSAGE KIT

Use these as editable drafts. Be specific, respectful and honest. Do not claim results or experience you do not have. Personalize each message and stop following up if the recipient declines.

1. WARM INTRODUCTION
Hello [Name], I’m learning to support [type of business] with [specific service]. I noticed [specific, respectful observation]. Would a short conversation about [possible deliverable] be useful? No pressure if it is not a priority.

2. REFERRAL INTRODUCTION
Hello [Name], [Referrer] suggested I reach out. I help with [service/deliverable]. If this is relevant, I can share a small sample and learn what you currently need. Would [time option] work for a brief conversation?

3. SOCIAL DM
Hi [Name], I liked your post about [topic]. I’m building a service around [specific task] for [audience]. I have one idea that may help with [observed need]. May I send it over?

4. EMAIL SUBJECT + INTRO
Subject: A small idea for [business]
Hello [Name], I’m [your name]. I noticed [specific public detail]. I support [audience] with [specific service]. If [need] is something you are working on, I’d be happy to ask a few questions and suggest a small, clearly scoped next step.

5. FOLLOW-UP (ONE TIME)
Hello [Name], just following up on my note about [service]. If it is not a fit or the timing is off, no worries. If you would like details, I can send a short outline.

6. AFTER A DISCOVERY CHAT
Thanks for explaining [need]. I’ll send a brief outline covering [deliverables], [timeline], [price/quote process] and what I’d need from you. Please let me know if I misunderstood anything.

7. SCOPE CLARIFIER
To make sure we agree, this project includes [deliverables] by [date]. It does not include [excluded items]. You’ll provide [inputs]. The price is [price] and includes [revision terms]. Is that accurate?

8. POLITE DECLINE / NOT A FIT
Thank you for considering me. I don’t think I can deliver [request] to the standard it deserves right now. I appreciate your time.

Personalization checklist: use the person’s name correctly; reference a real detail; describe one relevant service; make a small ask; be honest about your experience; proofread; respect a no.
