# Service Launch Checklist

## Choose and validate
- [ ] Choose one service from the book to practise first.
- [ ] Describe one type of customer and a need you can help with.
- [ ] Speak to a few potential customers; ask open questions and do not lead them.
- [ ] Check that people actually understand and care about the proposed service.

## Build the skill and sample
- [ ] Learn the tool’s current pricing, access rules, terms and data practices.
- [ ] Practise with sample or permissioned information.
- [ ] Create one sample deliverable and label it clearly as a sample.
- [ ] Review accuracy, spelling, accessibility, privacy and rights.
- [ ] Ask a trusted reviewer for specific feedback.

## Define the offer
- [ ] Write the deliverables in plain language.
- [ ] Set scope, exclusions, timeline, client inputs and revision limits.
- [ ] Calculate your time and costs before setting a price.
- [ ] Create a basic written proposal and payment process.
- [ ] Confirm relevant legal, tax and consumer requirements for your situation.

## Find and serve customers
- [ ] Make a small list of relevant businesses using public contact details appropriately.
- [ ] Personalize a short message; do not bulk spam.
- [ ] Track contact date, response and next step.
- [ ] Confirm scope before starting.
- [ ] Deliver and request feedback without pressure.
- [ ] Review what worked and choose one improvement.

This is an editable checklist for learning and planning. It is not legal, tax or financial advice and does not promise income.
